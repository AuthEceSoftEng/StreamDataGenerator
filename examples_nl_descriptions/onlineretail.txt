I want a very simple data stream generator about customer activity on an online retail platform.
It should include three features: age as an integer uniformly sampled between 18 and 70, session_time
as a float drawn from a Gaussian distribution with mean 20 and standard deviation 8, and device_type
as a categorical value chosen uniformly from mobile, desktop, or tablet. The target variable is
purchase, which is true when session_time is greater than 15 and age is below 60. I want two sudden drifts.
The first drift affects session_time, where after the drift the distribution changes from Gaussian(20,
8) to Gaussian(35, 10) to represent increased engagement after a platform redesign. The second drift
affects purchase, where after the drift the rule becomes slightly stricter (indicating e.g. external
market economic recession) so that a purchase only happens when session_time is greater than 20 and age
is below 55.
