I want a data stream generator about customer activity on an e-commerce analytics platform. It should include five features: age as an integer uniformly sampled between 18 and 70, session_time as a float drawn from a Gaussian distribution with mean 20 and standard deviation 8, browsing_depth as a float drawn from a Gaussian distribution with mean 5 and standard deviation 2, response_time as a float drawn from a Gaussian distribution with mean 2.0 and standard deviation 0.5, and device_type as a categorical value chosen uniformly from mobile, desktop, or tablet. The target variable is purchase, which is true when session_time is greater than 15 and age is below 60. I want four drifts. The first drift is sudden and affects session_time, where after the drift the distribution changes from Gaussian(20, 8) to Gaussian(35, 10) to represent increased engagement after a platform redesign. The second drift is incremental on response_time, where the mean shifts linearly from 2.0 to 4.5 to represent server load degrading response time during infrastructure scaling. The third drift is recurring on response_time, where the mean periodically spikes to 6.0 during holiday periods (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas) to represent traffic surges overwhelming the servers. The fourth drift is gradual on the target purchase, where the rule transitions over a number of steps from the default rule (session_time > 15 and age < 60) to a stricter rule (session_time > 25 and age < 55) to represent a seasonal pattern that progressively tightens the purchase condition.
